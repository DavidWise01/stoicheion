# RQT-183 — Discriminant Anti-Isometry and Extension Criterion

**2026-11-06 — exact original-source audit.** Uses the original RQT-133 operator and RQT-122 Gram matrix, archived in `research/rqt/source/`. Verified with exact SymPy arithmetic.

## Frozen data

M is the even unimodular rank-22 lattice of signature (3,19), h=(1,1,1,1,0^18) is primitive with h²=4, K=h^perp∩M has determinant -4 and A_K=K*/K=Z/4. Set t=e0-h/4, of order four modulo K, with t²=-9/4. The discriminant quadratic value is q_K([t])=-1/4 modulo 2Z. On Zh, q([h/4])=+1/4. The glue is generated by ([h/4],[t]) and e0=h/4+t.

## The extension criterion

The only isometries of the cyclic discriminant form (Z/4,-1/4) are multiplication by +1 and -1.

Given f∈O(K) and epsilon∈{+1,-1}, the rational orthogonal direct-sum map on Qh⊕(K⊗Q) extending h↦epsilon*h and f on K preserves the original integral overlattice M if and only if the induced action fbar on A_K satisfies

**fbar([t])=epsilon*[t]**.

Equivalently, f(t)-epsilon*t is an integral vector in K.

Proof: the primitive index-four overlattice M is obtained from Zh⊕K by adjoining e0=h/4+t. Under the proposed map, e0 maps to epsilon*h/4+f(t). Modulo Zh⊕K the original gluing subgroup is generated by ([h/4],[t]); membership of this image requires fbar([t])=epsilon*[t]. For ε=±1 the induced gluing automorphism is invertible, hence preserving it gives an integral isometry of M.

The deliberately failing map is F=2*(h*(h^T G)/4)-I. It fixes h, negates K, and maps e0 to h/2-e0, which is not integral.

The root reflection is F=I+v*(v^T G), v=e20∈K and v²=-2; it preserves h, G and the discriminant class.
