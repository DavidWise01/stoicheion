# RQT-184 — Stabilizer image and reflection structure

**2026-11-06 | Original RQT-122 Gram and RQT-133 operator**

Let M be the original even unimodular lattice with primitive h²=4 and K=h⊥∩M. The discriminant group A_K≅Z/4 has generator [t] with t=e0−h/4 and q(t)=−1/4 mod 2Z.

**Exact theorem:** the discriminant-sign homomorphism ρ:O(K)→O(A_K,q)≅{±1} is onto, since −I_K acts by −1. The RQT-183 gluing criterion identifies kerρ with O(M)_h, the integral isometries of M that fix h. Because −I_K is central and maps to −1, **O(K)≅O(M)_h×C₂**.

## Root witnesses

For the original source matrix, v=e20 and w=e21 are in K, and satisfy v²=w²=−2, ⟨v,w⟩=+1. The integral root reflections R_v=I+v(vᵀG) and R_w=I+w(wᵀG) satisfy R_v²=R_w²=(R_vR_w)³=I. Hence they generate an A₂-type S₃ subgroup of the stabilizer, with trivial action on A_K.

## Seven exact tests

Identity, X, R_v, R_w, R_vR_w, −I_M, and rational nonextension J=ha/2−I (a=hᵀG). The first six are integral and have matching h/discriminant signs; the last fixes h while negating K, and fails integrality. All seven passed the exact audit.

## Scope

This establishes the discriminant image, the central splitting, and an explicit root reflection subgroup. **It does not establish that all of O(M)_h is generated by (−2)-root reflections.** The original matrices and executable test are archived alongside this note.
